# Tagging rubric for APT Bulletin items

Each item gets zero or more **committee tags** and one or more **discipline tags**. Tags are suggestions made from the title, the APT index subject headings (`topics`) and the item type; nobody read the full articles. Tag what the piece is substantially *about*, not every word in the title. Two or three committee tags is common; tag none when nothing fits (e.g. an obituary, a general history of APT).

## Committee tags (APT technical committees, plus disaster as a PETC subgroup)

| Tag | Committee | Tag when the piece is substantially about |
|---|---|---|
| `DOC` | Technical Committee on Documentation (TC-Doc) | Recording and documenting buildings, sites and landscapes: measured drawings, HABS/HAER, photography, photogrammetry, laser scanning, GIS, BIM/HBIM, inventories and surveys, condition surveys and glossaries, archival/historical research methods, data management and digital records. |
| `MAT` | Technical Committee for Materials (TCM) | Specific building materials or assemblies: their history and manufacture, properties, deterioration, testing and analysis, cleaning, treatment, repair and replacement (masonry, stone, brick, mortar, terra cotta, concrete, wood, metals, glass, plaster, paint and finishes, roofing, windows, wallpaper, flooring, sealants, coatings, consolidants). |
| `PETC` | Preservation Engineering Technical Committee | Structural behavior, analysis, assessment, stabilization and repair; foundations; historic engineering structures and systems (bridges, trusses, vaults, early iron/steel/concrete frames, mills); structural NDE and load testing; history of structural engineering. |
| `CODES` | Technical Committee on Codes and Standards | Building, fire, life-safety and accessibility codes; standards and specifications (ASTM, Secretary of the Interior's Standards); guidelines and charters for practice. |
| `MH` | Technical Committee on Modern Heritage (TC-MH) | Modern and recent-past heritage, roughly 1920s onward: modernist buildings and landscapes, mid-century and postwar materials and systems (curtain walls, aluminum, plastics, early concrete shells, prefabrication). |
| `SP` | Technical Committee for Sustainable Preservation (TC-SP) | Sustainability, energy performance and retrofit, embodied carbon, climate change impacts and adaptation, building environment and moisture/HVAC as environmental performance, green building and rating systems. |
| `DIS` | Disaster (subgroup of PETC, tagged separately) | Natural and human-made disasters and hazards (earthquake, flood, hurricane, tornado, wildfire, major fire, war/conflict), emergency response and recovery, risk and vulnerability assessment for hazards, resilience and mitigation. |

## Discipline tags

At least one for every content item (not for letters or editorials whose subject is unclear).

| Tag | Tag when the piece's main lens is |
|---|---|
| `ENG` | Engineering: structural, mechanical or building-systems analysis, testing and quantitative performance, engineering history, materials engineering. |
| `ARCH` | Architecture: design, architectural history, building typologies, rehabilitation/adaptive use design, planning, landscape architecture, practice and project delivery. |
| `CONS` | Conservation: conservation treatment and conservation science of materials and finishes, preservation philosophy and policy, heritage management, maintenance, collections and interiors care. |

## Confidence

`high` when the title and topics make the tags clear; `low` when the title is vague (one or two words, a place name, a book title) and the tags are a guess.
